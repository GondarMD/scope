Pattern 01: Context Creep

Intent

Prevent exposure from increasing unnoticed as an AI system accumulates additional context through incremental integrations.

Problem

AI systems rarely become more consequential through a single major change.

Instead, they accumulate access to additional data sources over time, each justified independently. While each integration appears low risk, together they can substantially change the consequences of future decisions.

Traditional governance tends to review changes in authority while overlooking gradual changes in exposure.

Forces / Trade-offs

Pressure Counter-pressure
Better context improves system performance More context increases potential exposure
Integrations deliver incremental business value Accumulated integrations change system risk
Authority appears unchanged Exposure changes continuously
Teams optimise locally Governance must evaluate globally

This tension is what creates Context Creep.

Applicability

Use this pattern whenever:

  • AI systems consume multiple organisational data sources.
  • Context is expected to expand over time.
  • Future authority increases are likely.
  • Integrations are owned by different teams.
  • Governance reviews occur less frequently than technical changes.

Authority Stage(s)

Primary: Recommend Also relevant: Assist and Constrain

Although authority may remain static, this pattern explains why exposure should still be reassessed.

Actors

  • AI System
  • System Owner
  • Data Owners
  • Governance Reviewers
  • Scope Ledger Custodian

Interaction

  1. Initial deployment with limited context.
  2. Additional data source integrated.
  3. Performance improves.
  4. Another integration follows.
  5. Context expands further.
  6. Exposure silently increases.
  7. Authority expansion requested.
  8. Governance discovers it is evaluating a fundamentally different system.

SCOPE Interpretation

Context Creep illustrates one of SCOPE's central distinctions:

Authority and exposure evolve independently.

Authority determines what the system is permitted to do. Exposure reflects what is at risk if the system is wrong. A system can accumulate significant exposure while exercising exactly the same authority. This is why SCOPE treats context expansion as an exposure event rather than merely a technical enhancement.

Governance Implications

Governance should:

  • Treat meaningful context expansion as a review trigger.
  • Compare requested authority against current—not historical—exposure.
  • Record significant context changes in the Scope Ledger.
  • Require reassessment whenever accumulated exposure materially exceeds the exposure profile under which the current authority was approved.

Failure Modes

Common misconceptions that drive failure modes seen in this pattern:

  • Reviewing only authority changes.
  • Assuming unchanged authority means unchanged risk.
  • Treating integrations as purely technical work.
  • Failing to maintain an up-to-date Scope Ledger.
  • Allowing multiple small changes to bypass governance individually.

Example

A clinical triage assistant:

  • starts with a narrow role: reading symptoms captured during patient intake and suggesting a priority level for a clinician to review. Every recommendation is reviewed before anything happens.
  • The system proves useful, so its context grows. It gains access to medical history. Then medication records. Pathology results. Previous admissions. Later, clinician notes, allergies and data from wearable devices.
  • Months later, the organisation proposes a seemingly modest change: allow the assistant to route low-risk patients directly into standard treatment pathways.

While the increase in authority appears modest, the assistant's decisions are now informed by a far richer view of each patient.

Equally, the possible consequences of a system error (e.g. misinterpreting a pathology trend, overlooking a drug interaction, etc.), are more severe (e.g. a patient may be directed down the wrong treatment pathway before a clinician ever reviews the case).

The assistant hasn't simply become more capable. It has become more consequential.
